How it works

From tenant call to owner-ready report.

A four-step loop designed so the property manager touches the job exactly twice — at intake and at sign-off.

01

You log a job

Email, portal, or one tap from your PMS. We acknowledge in minutes — never a black hole. Or send the tenant straight to our hotline; we'll loop you in.

02

We attend & assess

Tenant booked direct (we handle the calls), licensed plumber on site within the agreed window, photos and scope documented before any work begins.

03

Quote or fix on the spot

Under your authorisation limit? Done. Over? Quote in your inbox before we touch a tool, with photos, options, and a recommended path.

04

Report & invoice

Owner-ready PDF report with photos, scope, parts, and warranty. Invoice to the agency or owner — your call. Closed in 24 hours, every job.
