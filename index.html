Common questions

No-BS answers.

Not listed? Call (03) 9000 0000 — a real plumber picks up, 24/7. No voicemail, no robots.

Do you actually arrive in 45 minutes, or is that marketing?

Across inner Melbourne (Port Phillip, Yarra, Boroondara, Stonnington, Glen Eira, Moreland, Darebin) our average is 38 minutes. If we pass 45, your invoice is $50 less — automatically. We don't need you to ask.

What's the call-out fee?

Zero. No fuel surcharge, no weekend loading, no after-hours levy. You pay for the job, not for us turning up. If you don't like the quote, we leave — you owe nothing.

Are you licensed for gas work?

Yes — fully licensed by the Victorian Building Authority (VBA licence 45821). All gas work is certified, logged in our SafetyCulture system, and compliance certificates issued within 24 hours of the job.

Do you handle strata / body corporate work?

Yes — we hold ongoing contracts with 40+ property management firms across Melbourne. Itemised invoicing, multi-unit quoting, compliance reporting all handled as standard. Ask for our property manager pack.

What if the problem comes back?

If it fails inside 12 months for the same issue, we come back free. No quibbling, no excess, no "well, technically." The warranty is written on your invoice.

Weekends and public holidays?

24 hours, 7 days, every public holiday — including Christmas Day. Same rates. Same guarantee. Same on-call team. No subcontractors.
