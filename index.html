Common questions

The stuff PMs ask first.

Do you bill the tenant or the agency?
Always the agency. The invoice comes through to your trust inbox in whatever format you need (PDF, MYOB-ready CSV, Console-import). We don't chase tenants for anything, ever.
What's your service area?
Melbourne's south-east is our zone: Berwick, Cranbourne, Pakenham, Narre Warren, Officer, Hampton Park, Frankston, Mornington and out to Dandenong. We'll travel for established PM partners — just ask.
How fast on an emergency call?
45 minutes on average in the core zone, 90 minutes peak. After-hours and weekend calls go to Brendan's mobile — a real plumber, not a triage centre. We hold parts on every van for the 12 most common emergency calls.
Do you do minimum-standards compliance?
Yes — full 15-point Vic rental check, written report with pass/fail on each item, rectification quote in the same format. Most new managements get it knocked over in a single visit.
What about quoted work over $500?
Anything over $500 ex-GST gets a written quote to the agency before we proceed. Quotes are itemised, with parts and labour separated, valid for 30 days, and include photos of the fault so the owner can see what they're paying for.
Are you fully licensed and insured?
Plumbing & gas licensed (Lic. 108814), public liability to $20m, workers comp current. Compliance certificates issued via VBA on every gas job. Happy to send paperwork through to your office before we onboard.
